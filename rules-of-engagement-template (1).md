# Authorized Security Lab — Rules of Engagement

## Written authorization
Name the lab owner, assessor, approved assets, and approval date. Never test a system that is absent from the asset inventory.

## Permitted techniques
List the exact reconnaissance, validation, and reporting activities allowed in the isolated lab.

## Explicit exclusions
No denial of service, destructive payloads, persistence, credential reuse, third-party targets, or uncontrolled data extraction.

## Testing window and stop conditions
Define start/end time, emergency contact, rate limits, and conditions requiring immediate stop.

## Evidence handling
Store the minimum evidence, redact secrets and personal data, and define deletion timing.

## Reporting
For each finding record asset, severity rationale, reproduction steps, impact, evidence, and remediation.
